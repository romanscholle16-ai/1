Route at a glance

    Weight rules for the whole trip

    • Scrap-and-store at settlements. Anything you only want as building material goes into a workshop at Sanctuary, so don't haul it.
    • Carry by value per pound: Fusion Cores (keep a few), Stealth Boys, Mini Nukes, chems, ammo, pre-war money, and weapons only if they are legendary or you can use them. Leave heavy bulk junk and plain 10mm pistols, hunting rifles, and raider armor behind.
    • Perks that help: Strong Back and Pack Mule (Strength), Barter and Hacker/Local Leader style trade perks (Charisma), and Dogmeat or another companion as a mule. Use the Pip-Boy's weight sort to find your heaviest items first.
    • Legendary (star) enemies drop the rare weapons you want. When you see a glowing or star-marked enemy, fight it from range, then loot the legendary item and leave the junk.
